# Luex Life – Household Money feature sheet

Agreed 27 Oct 2026. Module 5 of 15 (parents only). Research: YNAB, Emma, Snoop, Goodbudget, Honeydue, UK open banking providers.

## Owner's decisions
1. Build every must-have and the niche items marked below. Ultra niche not chosen is a reference list only.
2. Fix every complaint in the complaints list.
3. Parents only (the database enforces it).
4. No live bank connections: statement import (CSV/OFX) plus quick manual entry. Live connections could come later as a paid extra.
5. "Just mine": each parent can keep accounts and payments private from the other parent.
6. Renewals: amounts live here; renewal dates and documents live in Cars and Renewals; the two are linked.
7. No advice and no commission: facts, reminders and plain links only.
8. Credit score: record it yourself, with a reminder and plain links to free checking services (no commission).
9. Bill switching: "contract ends soon — shop around" reminders, price-rise alerts and plain comparison links (no commission).
10. Joint account: tracked in the app (both parents see and add to it); not a real bank account or card.
11. Push notifications only, as for the rest of the app.

## Must have
1. Monthly household budget: categories, planned against actual, left this month, carry-over.
2. Bills and regular payments: amount, how often, account, next due date; bills calendar; "due in the next 7 days"; push reminders.
3. Subscriptions: yearly cost, renewal date, free-trial-ending reminders, monthly and yearly totals.
4. Spending tracker: add a payment in seconds (amount, category, who paid, note); recurring items fill themselves in; Food's shop totals flow in.
5. Import a bank statement (CSV/OFX) from any UK bank: column matching remembered per bank, duplicates skipped, categories suggested and remembered per payee, check screen before saving.
6. Two parents, one picture: both see and edit; "who paid"; "just mine" accounts and payments.
7. Savings goals: target, date, progress, "£X a month to get there".
8. Clear reports: this month against last, by category, year so far, biggest changes.
9. Search and filter payments.
10. Export everything to CSV and PDF.
11. No ads, no deal-pushing, no referral banners.
12. Works offline: add a payment with no signal; it sends later.
13. Grandparent-proof: plain words ("money in", "money out", "left this month"), sensible default categories, no jargon.

## Niche (agreed)
1. Gift budgets per person per occasion, linked to the Calendar's wish lists.
2. Christmas and holiday pots.
3. Price-rise alerts on bills (a bill's amount changes on import).
4. Debts and loans: balance, payment, payoff date, overpayment "what if".
5. Payday view (payday to payday, including four-weekly pay).
6. Cash tracking (an envelope for cash).
7. Receipts: photograph and attach to a payment (on-phone scanning, as in Food).
8. Annual bills spread monthly ("£480 a year = £40 a month to set aside").
9. Child costs: total spent per child per year.
10. Pocket money totals from module 6 shown in the budget.
11. Weekly money summary push.

## Ultra niche chosen
1. Investments and pensions: values typed in when statements arrive; growth over time (no live prices).
2. Credit score: record it yourself, reminder to check, plain links to free services.
3. Better deals: contract-ending reminders, price-rise alerts, plain comparison links (no commission).
4. Multiple currencies: per account or payment, converted to pounds with daily European Central Bank rates (free public feed).
5. Joint account tracked in the app.
6. Mortgage calculator: repayments, overpayments, rate changes; an estimate, not advice.

## Not wanted
1. Settling up shared costs.
2. Net worth.
3. Category limit warnings.
4. Live bank connections (for now), tax returns, paying bills in the app, cryptocurrency.

## Complaints and fixes
1. Expensive, rising prices (YNAB) – included in the family price.
2. Weeks to learn (YNAB) – plain words, ready-made categories.
3. Bank sync drops, duplicates, late transactions (YNAB, Honeydue, Emma) – no live sync; imports skip duplicates.
4. No proper partner access (YNAB) – each parent has their own login, with "who paid".
5. No offline mode (YNAB) – add offline, sends later.
6. Upsells and promotional clutter (Emma) – none.
7. CSV-only exports (Emma) – CSV and PDF.
8. Category changes revert; can't delete categories (Honeydue) – fully editable, remembered per payee.
9. Bills wrongly marked overdue (Honeydue) – paid when ticked or when a matching payment is imported.
10. Can't ignore transfers or one-offs (Honeydue) – "not in the budget" on any payment.
11. Data kept only about 6 months (Honeydue) – kept until you delete it.
12. Unclear data sharing (Emma) – nothing shared; no third parties.

## Gaps owned by other modules
1. Pocket money and rewards – module 6 (totals shown here).
2. Renewal dates and documents – Cars and Renewals (amounts here, linked).
3. Food shop totals – Food (flow in).
4. Bills paperwork – Documents and Papers.
5. Holiday spending detail – Trips and Holidays (a pot here).
6. Gift ideas – the Calendar's wish lists (budgets here).

## Honest risks
1. Statement formats differ by bank: the column-matching step is needed the first time for each bank.
2. Money is the most sensitive data in the app: parents only, "just mine" privacy, and a stronger-model security review before launch; phone face ID or PIN lock once the store apps exist.
3. Exchange rates and mortgage figures are estimates.
