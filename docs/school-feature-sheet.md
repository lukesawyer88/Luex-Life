# Luex Life – School and Activities feature sheet

Agreed 27 Oct 2026. Module 7 of 15 (everyone). Research: ParentMail, Arbor, ClassDojo, Satchel One, Tapestry, Spond, TeamSnap.

## Owner's decisions
1. Simple on purpose: Luex Life simplifies and organises home and family life. Only the must-haves are built.
2. Schools and clubs choose their own apps and none can be connected, so Luex is the one place for what matters from all of them: what's on, what to bring, what's due and who's picking up. Things are typed in or photographed.
3. School is a simple view on top of the Calendar, not a second calendar: clubs and babysitting are ordinary Calendar events.
4. Decided the simple way (owner said go ahead): homework is a simple list; childcare is people and bookings; club fees are shown, not sent to Household Money; pickups have a weekly grid.
5. Push notifications only, as for the rest of the app.

## Built (must have)
1. Each child's school, class, teacher, school times and phone number.
2. The week at a glance per child: what to bring each day and school dinner or packed lunch.
3. Clubs and activities: day, time, place, kit and cost, on the Calendar automatically.
4. Pickups and drop-offs: the normal week, plus "change for one day" (the person now doing it is told).
5. Things to do: forms, money for trips, dress-up days and homework, with a due date, a tick and a photo of the letter.
6. Childcare and babysitters: names and numbers, and "Book them" puts it on the Calendar.
7. A morning summary at 7am: what to bring, clubs, who's picking up and what's due (parents hear about every child; a child about themselves; a grandparent or babysitter only about the runs they're doing).
8. Children's own "today" and their own homework list.
9. A "today at school" card on Home.

## Not built (could be added later)
1. School holiday cover planner.
2. Lift-sharing with other families.
3. Club fees into Household Money's bills.
4. Uniform sizes and what to buy.
5. Parents' evening times.
6. Exam timetable.
7. Reading log.

## Not possible
1. Connecting to ParentMail, Arbor, Satchel One or club apps (none offer it).

## Complaints and fixes
1. Too many apps and logins – one place, one login.
2. Can't add another child, or a child at school and a club – every child, school and club together.
3. No search; hundreds of messages – short items with due dates, not a message feed.
4. Inbox clutter – one morning summary.
5. Forms that don't say what to do – each item says what and by when.
6. Contact details shared with strangers – private to the household.
7. Payments failing – Luex reminds; nothing is paid in the app.

## Honest risks
1. Nothing comes in automatically: things are typed in or photographed ("forward an email" waits on the Luex email domain).
2. Teenagers may prefer their school's own homework app.

## Build status (27 Oct 2026)
Built and tested on a copy; not live yet (waiting for the owner's yes). The page is kept out of the menu until the database is live.

Checks: 29 School database checks (every other module's checks still passing), 81 one-screen checks (two parents, a child and a grandparent at phone, tablet and laptop size), 17 journey tests (setting up a school, the week, a club onto the Calendar, trip money, Today, the Home card, a grandparent changing a pickup and the new person being told, a child's homework). An independent security review found 4 problems (a private Calendar event could appear in the morning summary; editing a pickup note re-sent messages; a child could be put on the school run; removing a letter photo blocked ticking the task); all fixed and tested.
