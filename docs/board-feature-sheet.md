# Luex Life – Family Board feature sheet

Agreed 27 Oct 2026. Module 3 of 15. Research: FamilyWall, Cozi, FamilyGoal, Tinybeans, FamilyAlbum, iCloud Shared Albums, Qeepsake, Day One Shared Journals, WhatsApp family groups, Messenger Kids, OurGroceries, Listonic.

## Owner's decisions
1. Build every must-have and the niche items marked below. Ultra niche is a reference list only.
2. Fix every complaint in the complaints list.
3. The Board is about what WhatsApp is bad at: full-quality photos in one album, lists and notes that stay put, memories, and safe chat for children. Chat stays simple.
4. Storage: 50 GB per family, videos up to 3 minutes, a clear warning at 80%.
5. The shopping list lives in Food (module 4). Every other list lives on the Board.
6. Children's chats: a setting per child, "Parents can read this child's chats". On by default for under-13s; parents choose for teenagers. The child can always see whether it's on.
7. No end-to-end encryption. Messages are encrypted in transit and on the server and are private to the family; the privacy policy says plainly that Luex could technically read them.
8. Location is a one-off "Where are you?" request, never always-on (see niche 17).
9. Push notifications only, grouped, with mute per chat and quiet hours from day one.

## Must have
1. Family chat: one whole-family chat, plus private chats between two people and small groups (for example parents only).
2. Photos and videos in messages, private to the family.
3. Unread counts and notifications per chat; mute per chat; quiet hours (per person, set by a parent for children).
4. Replies, emoji reactions, edit and delete your own messages, pin a message.
5. "Seen by" on each message.
6. Family album at full quality: originals kept, never shrunk; every upload shows a tick or a clear retry.
7. Save the original photo or video back to your device.
8. Shared lists: everyone sees every list; ticks update for everyone straight away; drag to reorder; give an item to a person.
9. Notes: pinned notes on the board, like notes on the fridge.
10. Board front page: pinned notes, newest photos, latest messages and things needing you, on one screen.
11. Children kept safe: children only ever talk to their own family; parents can remove any message; each child's features can be switched on or off.
12. Family goals: shared goals with steps and progress, and a celebration when one is reached.
13. Journal and memories: a memory with a date, photos and who was there; milestones per child with their age.
14. Search across messages, lists, notes and memories.
15. Readable without a signal; messages send when the signal is back.
16. Download everything at any time (photos and a free PDF of memories).
17. No ads, no surprise paywalls.
18. Polls in any chat or on the board.
19. Grandparent-proof: big text, few buttons, an obvious send button.
20. Undo on delete; parents can tidy anything.

## Niche (agreed)
1. Voice notes.
2. Daily journal questions for children. Parents switch them on or off per child, choose the time and can add their own questions.
3. Year book PDF (free), plus printed books through a print-on-demand partner posting straight to the family; compare real partner prices before choosing.
4. Turn a message into a calendar event, a list item or a reminder.
5. Announcements: a parent post everyone taps "Got it" on.
6. Read-only album links for relatives without a login (time-limited, like the Calendar's share links).
7. Polls include a "pick a date" type; the winning date becomes a calendar event in one tap.
8. Shout-outs (kudos wall).
9. Family bucket list.
10. Scan children's drawings straight into an album.
11. List templates (school morning, weekly basics).
12. Stars for goals (spent in Pocket Money and Rewards, module 6).
13. Remind me about this message later.
14. Stickers and a colour per child: a built-in set of free-licence stickers only, no outside GIF service.
15. Tag a person (@name): they get a notification and the post joins their memories.
16. Parents can see children's journal answers unless they choose otherwise.
17. "Where are you?": anyone can ask another family member; that person gets a notification and taps "Share my location" once. It shows as a pin in the chat with "Open in Maps", and the location disappears after one hour. Never shared without a tap. Works in the web app (no background tracking).

## Not wanted
1. "On this day" memories.
2. Parent approval of children's posts.

## Ultra niche (reference only, not built)
1. Video and voice calls – later.
2. End-to-end encryption – dropped.
3. Face recognition – no.
4. In-house printing – covered by the print partner.
5. Always-on location sharing – replaced by "Where are you?".
6. Disappearing messages – no.
7. Posting to Facebook or Instagram – no.
8. TV slideshow – no.
9. Translating messages – no.
10. Hashtags – no (tagging people only, niche 15).

## Complaints and fixes
1. No unread markers, posts missed (FamilyWall) – unread counts on every chat, list and album.
2. Notifications stopped working (FamilyWall) – the Calendar's tested push service; per-chat on/off and quiet hours.
3. Lists hidden from others, can't reorder (FamilyWall) – everyone sees every list; drag to reorder.
4. Photos shrunk, silent upload failures, album caps (iCloud) – originals kept; tick or retry on every upload.
5. Ads and storage paywalls (Tinybeans, OurGroceries) – no ads; one clear family allowance.
6. Memories held hostage, expensive books (Qeepsake) – free export any time; printing optional.
7. WhatsApp family-group chaos – photos go to the album automatically; lists and notes stay pinned; mute instead of leaving.
8. No silent or bedtime mode (Messenger Kids) – quiet hours per child.
9. Facebook account needed, only two managers (Messenger Kids) – any parent manages children; no outside account.
10. Can't remove a child's message (Messenger Kids) – parents can remove anything.
11. Captions lost (Qeepsake) – captions saved with the photo.
12. One photo per entry on free plans (Day One) – no per-entry limit.

4. Packing lists – Trips and Holidays.
5. Wi-Fi password, passport numbers – Documents and Papers (parents only).
6. Birthdays and countdowns – Calendar.

## Honest risks
1. Full-quality video costs real money to store and serve: the allowance and 3-minute cap matter.
2. iPhone videos (HEVC .mov) won't play in some Windows browsers: keep the original and show "Download to watch"; conversion is on the to-do list (needs a paid service).
3. Notification volume: grouping, mute and quiet hours are must-haves, not extras.
4. Children's chat is a safeguarding area: ask the same adviser as for Kids' Space.
