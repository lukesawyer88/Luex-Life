# Luex Life – features trimmed for simplicity (27 Oct 2026)

The owner's rule: Luex Life simplifies and organises home and family life. Anything that went beyond that was trimmed.
Nothing was deleted: each one is switched off by default and a parent can switch it back on in Settings → More features.
Any of them can be brought back as a standard feature later.

## Family Calendar
(Booking references and countdowns on events became standard on 27 Oct 2026: Bookings and Trips rely on them.)
1. Change history on events (a plain "Added by" line stays; change alerts still go out).
2. Comments on events.
3. Weather (also needs a paid weather plan before launch).
4. Routine timers and "finished early".
6. Print the week.
7. The Christmas countdown in December (countdowns on events are standard).
8. Handover notes for a day.
9. Also changed: the two kinds of share link (babysitter and outsider) are now one "Share a link".

## Family Board
1. "Seen by" on messages.
2. Pinned messages.
3. Journal and memories: memories, milestones, daily journal questions, the year book and printed books, tagging into memories.
4. Family goals (goals with steps and stars).
5. Shout-outs.
6. Bucket list.
7. Stickers.
8. Remind me later on a message.
9. Pick-a-date polls (plain polls stay).
10. Scan a drawing (plain photo upload stays).

## Food
1. Fridge and cupboard stock, use-by dates and running-low (a simple freezer list stays).
2. Scanning: barcodes, receipts and cookbook pages.
3. Calories and nutrition.
4. Big-meal cooking timelines (Christmas dinner).
5. Meal ideas from what you have, leftovers and batch cooking.
6. Recipe collections, notes and "made it" history.
7. Family ratings on recipes.
8. Giving a shopping item to a person.
9. Also changed: packed lunches moved inside the meal plan (no longer their own tab).

## Household Money
1. Other currencies.
2. Investments, pensions, credit score and the mortgage calculator.
3. Debt overpayment "what if" (simple debts stay, in Savings).
4. Four-weekly pay periods (calendar month and payday stay).
5. Carry-over of unspent money.
6. Receipt photos.
7. Child costs per year.
8. Detailed reports, filters, PDF export and remembered payees (a simple "this month against last" stays, plus CSV download).
9. Also changed: tabs reduced to This month, Payments, Bills, Savings.

## Rewards and Pocket Money
1. Habits for anyone (streaks, counters, weekly targets, buddies and cheers).
2. Treat jar for grown-ups.
3. Shared rewards.
4. Give pot.
5. Interest and matching on savings.
6. Advances.
7. Teen mode.
8. Swapping stars for money.
9. Weekly bonus.
10. Taking off money or stars (deductions).
11. Tooth fairy and birthday money records (gifts stay).
12. Money tips for children.
13. Photo proof on chores and jobs.
14. History as a PDF (CSV stays).
15. Also changed: the "for me / for us / for the children" switches removed; tabs are Children, Waiting, Jobs, History for parents and "My stars and money", Jobs for children.

## Cars and Renewals (folded into Documents, 27 Oct 2026)
Not a module of its own: Documents already tracks MOT, car tax, insurance and breakdown cover with reminders, Money tracks contracts ending, and Home Care has "Service the car". Documents' "Renewals" tab now shows all of these in one list with a small icon for each kind (car, papers, contracts, home jobs).
Could be added later:
1. A page per car (MOT, tax, insurance and service for each vehicle together).
2. Fuel and mileage logs.
3. Carpool beyond the Calendar's driver and School's runs.
